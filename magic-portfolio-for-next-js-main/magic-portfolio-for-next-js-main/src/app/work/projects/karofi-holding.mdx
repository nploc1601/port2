## Overview

At Karofi Holding, I acted as a Growth Marketer focused on digital user acquisition and performance campaign management for advanced smart home appliances.

## Strategy & Key Insights

- **Product Positioning**: Focused on understanding user pain points to craft tailored copywriting and creative angles.
- **Automation Loops**: Developed internal data collection pipelines to shorten the feedback loop and optimize performance budgets.
- **Audience Targeting**: Analyzed demographic behavior to focus ad spend on high-potential customer personas.

## Outcome

- Reduced campaign cost-per-lead while increasing lead quality.
- Enabled quicker product delivery decisions by establishing swift tracking insights.
