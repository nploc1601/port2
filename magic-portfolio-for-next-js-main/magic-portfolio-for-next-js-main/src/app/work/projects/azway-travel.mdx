## Overview

At Azway Travel, I served as the Performance Marketing Lead, orchestrating user acquisition, campaign execution, and full-funnel tracking. The goal was to optimize campaign CPA, increase CTR, and design scalable marketing infrastructure.

## Key Actions & Strategy

- **Campaign Execution**: Managed Meta, Google, and TikTok Ads budgets, prioritizing high-intent travel queries and custom lookalike audiences.
- **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)**: Developed high-converting Landing Pages (LDPs) via LadiPage for key travel segments including Sunworld, Team Building, and Ha Long Cruise.
- **Tracking & Dashboards**: Built real-time conversion trackers, combining Google Analytics 4, GTM, and custom webhooks to bridge lead-to-booking data gaps.

## Key Outcomes

- **CTR**: **3.93%** (benchmark >2.0%)
- **Lead-to-Qualified CVR**: **31.0%** (benchmark: 20-30%)
- **Qualified-to-Booking CVR**: **10.5%** (benchmark: >7.0%)
- **Creative Diversification**: Produced multi-format assets testing diverse video angles and carousel tour options.
