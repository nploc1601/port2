## Overview

Developed a comprehensive digital launch plan for **Livotec**'s smart home appliances, tracking awareness and performance metrics.

## Key Tactics

- **Performance Funnel Setup**: Standardized tracking hooks using GA4 to track users from initial video ads to landing page product selection.
- **Retail Integrations**: Directed high-intent leads to key partner distributor stores, building cross-channel attribution.
- **Dynamic Creatives**: A/B tested multiple localized video ad formats to match consumer lifestyle pain points.
