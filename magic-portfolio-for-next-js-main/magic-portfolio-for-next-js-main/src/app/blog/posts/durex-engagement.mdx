## Overview

This case study outlines a digital engagement proposal for **Durex**, crafting social-first creative campaigns that generate viral buzz and seamless paths to purchase.

## Campaign Highlights

- **Interactive Quizzes**: Designing anonymous educational quizzes to address product selection and build brand trust.
- **E-commerce Integrations**: Bundling quiz completion with exclusive discount codes redeemable on Shopee and Lazada.
- **Influencer Collaborations**: Leveraging micro-influencers to spark relatable conversations and destigmatize sexual health.
